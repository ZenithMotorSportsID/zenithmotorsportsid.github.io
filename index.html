Why Zenith

Zenith means the peak.

I'm Brian, 21, and I've spent my whole life in Idaho Falls. I know this community, and I'm passionate about bringing a real car community to Southeast Idaho.

I'm a Chevy performance guy. I bought my dream car, a Camaro SS, but I love all things American muscle, especially a nice throaty V8. Every platform is welcome here…

…unless it's an EcoBoost or electric. 😉

That's what we're building: real meets, real cars, built from the ground up in the 208.
